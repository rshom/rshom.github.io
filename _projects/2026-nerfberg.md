title: NeRFBerg
published: true
abstract: >-
  Iceberg reports still reduce each iceberg to a height and width class.
  This project reconstructs iceberg sails in 3D from commercial
  off-the-shelf (COTS) drone video using neural radiance fields (NeRF),
  which gives surface area and volume directly. On icebergs in Disko Bay,
  Greenland, the NeRF reconstructions were more accurate than traditional
  photogrammetry across every measured quantity. The work started as a
  URI AI Lab undergraduate project.
