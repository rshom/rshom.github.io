I earned a doctorate from URI in Ocean Engineering after defending my
dissertation, “Advances in Deep-Sea Imaging and Soft Robotics”. My
work, under Dr. Brennan Phillips explored low-cost technology
development and application to deep sea research problems. In addition
to numerous collaborations, I led projects developing a soft robotic
drive and deep sea imaging software and platforms. The work performed
in my graduate studies continues to drive my ongoing research goals.


Dissertation text is available here:
