Our team is developing low-cost tools and methodologies for
  simultaneous surface and subsurface reconstruction of Arctic sea ice
  pressure ridges to determine ice thickness and geometry utilizing
  portable ROV and UAV based photogrammetry and AI based
  reconstruction methods.
These measurements are vital for forecasting ice coverage and
  movement, but subsurface data is rare due to expense and collection
  difficulty.
Our methodology significantly reduces cost and resources required
  for subsurface data collection and directly matches subsurface to
  surface data.
Increased collection of correlated subsurface and surface ice
  topology data may lead to predictive algorithms capable of large
  area ice thickness forecasting using available surface based
  measurements.
