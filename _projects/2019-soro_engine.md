title: Deep-Sea Soft Robotic Drive
abstract: >-
  Soft robotic grippers can handle fragile deep-sea animals, but the
  pneumatic drives that power them on land fail at deep-sea pressures. This
  project built a pressure-compensated hydraulic drive that moves fluid
  directly from a servo-driven bellows to the actuator, with a 72 W peak
  draw. Prototypes ran on multiple ROVs to almost 4000 m and grasped and
  released a stalked tunicate, a Venus flytrap anemone, and a xenophyophore
  without harm. Work with Brennan Phillips and Stephen Licht at URI, with
  the Harvard Microrobotics Lab.
