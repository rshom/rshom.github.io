We developed a novel deep sea hydraulic drive for soft robotic manipulators.
Soft robotic manipulators can perform valuable tasks for deep sea research, however the standard pneumatic drives cannot be employed due to high ambient pressures.
Prototype drives were demonstrated on multiple deep sea platforms with various soft robotic manipulators.
This design allows soft robotic manipulators to be easily added to existing deep sea robotic platforms.
