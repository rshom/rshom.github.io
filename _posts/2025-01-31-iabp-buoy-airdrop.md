I joined the [International Arctic Buoy Programme](https://iabp.apl.uw.edu/) (IABP) on its January 2025 buoy deployment, flying with the Alaska Air National Guard on a C-17 out of Anchorage. Over 24 flight hours, buoys were air-dropped onto the sea ice across the Beaufort Sea to report weather and ice drift. This is IABP's work, coordinated by Ignatius Rigor at UW APL. I was along to help and learn the operation.
