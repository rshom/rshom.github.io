In 2025 I joined two field efforts with the [International Arctic Buoy Programme](https://iabp.apl.uw.edu/) (IABP), coordinated by Ignatius Rigor at UW APL. In January, I flew with the Alaska Air National Guard on a C-17 out of Anchorage while buoys were air-dropped onto the sea ice across the Beaufort Sea, 24 flight hours in all, to report weather and ice drift. In April, during field work in Utqiaġvik, Alaska, I joined an IABP and UIC Science Support outreach visit to Hopson Middle School, where students worked through a sea ice lesson in the classroom and on the ice and set out wooden boats from IABP's Float Your Boat program to drift with the ice. Both are IABP's work; I was along to help and learn.
