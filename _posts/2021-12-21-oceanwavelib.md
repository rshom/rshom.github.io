A MATLAB toolbox that turns standard ocean wave spectra (monochromatic, Pierson-Moskowitz, and JONSWAP) into surface and subsurface time series of elevation, velocity, and acceleration. I wrote it for the [wave-powered profiling float]({{ '/wave-powered-float' | relative_url }}) simulations.

- Code: [github.com/rshom/matlab-oceanwavelib](https://github.com/rshom/matlab-oceanwavelib)
