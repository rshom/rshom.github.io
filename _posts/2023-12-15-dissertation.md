I defended my dissertation in Ocean Engineering at URI under [Brennan Phillips](https://web.uri.edu/uril/). It covers three projects in deep-sea robotics for platforms limited in size, weight, power, and cost (SWAP-C): a pressure-compensated hydraulic drive for soft robotic actuators, open-source software for the DEEPi camera, and a multi-view camera array built from networked DEEPi cameras.
