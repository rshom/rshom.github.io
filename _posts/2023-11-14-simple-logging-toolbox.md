A small, fast MATLAB logging toolbox. Logging statements stay in the code, and a single level setting in `log.m` turns them on or off. Options are hard-coded instead of branched, which trades convenience for speed.

- Code: [github.com/rshom/Simple-Logging-Toolbox](https://github.com/rshom/Simple-Logging-Toolbox)
