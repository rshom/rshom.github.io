During field work in Utqiaġvik, Alaska, in April 2025, I joined an outreach visit by the [International Arctic Buoy Programme](https://iabp.apl.uw.edu/) (IABP) and UIC Science Support to Hopson Middle School. Students worked through a sea ice lesson in the classroom and on the ice, and set out wooden boats from IABP's Float Your Boat program to drift with the ice. The program is IABP's, run by Ignatius Rigor at UW APL.
