# About

**Marine Research Associate**, Graduate School of Oceanography, University of Rhode Island<br>
**Adjunct Professor**, Department of Ocean Engineering, University of Rhode Island

<img class="headshot" src="{{ '/assets/img/shomberg.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Russ Shomberg">

I am a marine robotics engineer developing sensing and data tools for
oceanographic and polar research, the local marine environment, and
dual-use defense applications. My current research focuses on acoustic
and optical sensing, embedded systems, and data analysis with artificial
intelligence. I build for low cost and field deployment, so that groups
without a research vessel budget can still collect useful data. I have
over 12 cumulative months of field work, flight hours, and sea time.

Before returning to URI, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the
[Maryland Robotics Center](https://robotics.umd.edu/), University of
Maryland. I earned an MS and PhD in Ocean Engineering from URI under
Dr. Brennan Phillips and a BS in Mechanical Engineering from Boston
University. Before graduate school I served as a Cryptologic Warfare
Officer in the US Navy.

## Research

- **Polar ice reconstruction** ([NeRFBerg]({{ '/nerfberg' | relative_url }})). Aerial and underwater imagery and sonar
  of icebergs and sea ice ridges, reconstructed in 3D with neural
  radiance fields (NeRF) and Gaussian splatting. Field work in Greenland
  and the Antarctic Peninsula, plus air-deployed ice buoys with the
  International Arctic Buoy Programme.
- **Acoustic sensing.** Side-scan sonar processing and 3D sonar
  reconstruction, including survey work in Narragansett Bay.
- **Low-cost deep-sea tools.** The DEEPi camera system (tested past
  5000 m), liquid-filled cameras, and a hydraulic drive for deep-sea soft
  robotic manipulators (tested to about 3000 m from R/V *Falkor*).

## Teaching and Mentoring
